# 🏛️ AtlasTime: Forge Your Discipline

**AtlasTime** is a minimalist, gamified habit-tracking application designed to help users quit bad habits. Inspired by apps like Quitzilla, it focuses on visual progress through the growth of an inner "Spartan Warrior" and provides deep emotional insights through integrated journaling.

## ⚔️ The Spartan Growth System

As you maintain your streak, your character evolves to reflect your growing discipline:
- **0 - 7 Days (Infant):** A baby, representing the birth of a new path.
- **1 - 2 Weeks (Acolyte):** The boy is on his knees, beginning his journey.
- **2 - 4 Weeks (Squire):** He stands up, finding his footing.
- **1 - 2 Months (Trainee):** A young child practicing basic movements.
- **2 - 6 Months (Warrior):** A teenager, solid on his feet with sword and shield.
- **6+ Months (Legend):** A fully-armored adult Spartan soldier.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Real-time Tracking:** High-precision timers showing exactly how long you've remained disciplined.
- **Visual Statistics:** Progress graphs and "War Room" stats showing best streaks and relapse counts.
- **Journal & Feelings:** Link your emotional state to your progress. Record notes when you relapse to identify triggers.
- **Milestones & Trophies:** Earn "Hall of Trophies" awards at key intervals (24h, 3 days, 1 week, etc.).
- **PWA Ready:** Installable on Android and iOS with offline support and "App Shortcuts" (long-press icon).
- **Home Screen "Widget":** A special minimalist view designed to be used with web-widget utility apps.

## 📱 Installation (PWA)

1. Host the project on a public URL (Vercel, GitHub Pages, etc.).
2. Open the URL on your mobile browser.
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Use it like a native app!

## 🛠️ The "Widget" Workaround

Since standard PWAs don't support native OS widgets, AtlasTime includes a **Widget Mode**:
1. Go to **Settings** in the app.
2. Click **"Copy Widget URL"**.
3. Use a 3rd party app like **"Web Widget"** (Android) and paste that URL.
4. Set the refresh rate to 1 minute for a live home-screen Spartan!

## 🧪 Developer Mode
Enable **Developer Mode** in settings to access "Time Travel" buttons in the habit detail view. This allows you to jump forward 7 or 30 days to preview the Spartan's growth stages.
